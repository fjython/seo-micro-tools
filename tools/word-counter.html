FAQ

How does the word counter work?

Type or paste text into the box. Words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs are counted instantly in your browser.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Counting runs entirely in your browser with JavaScript. Nothing is sent to a server.

Does it count characters with spaces?

Yes. You get both character counts with and without spaces.
