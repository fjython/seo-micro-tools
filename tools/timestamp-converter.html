FAQ

What is a Unix timestamp?

A Unix timestamp is the number of seconds (or milliseconds) since 1970-01-01 00:00:00 UTC.

Seconds or milliseconds?

Both are supported. Values with 13+ digits are treated as milliseconds; shorter ones as seconds.

Is conversion private?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser with no server upload.
