FAQ

What can this tool remove?

Leading/trailing spaces, repeated spaces, blank lines, tabs, or all whitespace characters.

Will it change my line breaks?

Trim and collapse modes keep line structure. Remove blank lines and strip-all modes change newlines as described on each button.

Is text uploaded?

No. Cleaning runs locally in your browser.
