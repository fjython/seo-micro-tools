FAQ

What case formats are supported?

Uppercase, lowercase, title case, sentence case, camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, and kebab-case.

Is text sent to a server?

No. Conversion runs in your browser with JavaScript.

Can I convert code identifiers?

Yes. Use camel, Pascal, snake, or kebab modes for variable and CSS naming.
