FAQ

What is a URL slug?

A slug is the readable part of a URL path, usually lowercase words separated by hyphens, like my-blog-post.

How are accents handled?

Accented characters are normalized to ASCII equivalents when possible (é → e), then non-alphanumeric characters are removed.

Is text uploaded?

No. Slug generation runs entirely in your browser.
