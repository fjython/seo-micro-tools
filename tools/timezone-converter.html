FAQ

How do I convert between timezones?

Pick a source timezone and datetime, choose a target timezone, and click Convert. Results use your browser's Intl API.

Does it account for daylight saving?

Yes. Named IANA timezones automatically apply DST rules for the selected date.

Is an internet connection required?

Only to load the page. Conversion runs offline in the browser.
