FAQ

Are these tools free?

Yes. Every tool on this site is free to use in your browser with no signup.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens locally with JavaScript in your browser. Nothing is sent to a backend.

Do I need an internet connection after the page loads?

Once the page is loaded, the tools work offline. You only need a connection to open the page.
